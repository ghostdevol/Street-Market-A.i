    Inside the Board

    Five modules, each with one job. This is the contract architecture the jobsheet above is running — 13/13 tests, 228 KB Wasm.

    Settlement states — an obligation is not funds

    For non-NEAR jobs, the poster confirming the work done only moves the job to AwaitingSettlement. Paid-out stays at zero until the settlement layer reports the asset actually moved — with a transaction reference stored as evidence. NEAR jobs skip the question entirely: escrow was locked at posting, so release is just release.

    Pending→ AwaitingSettlement→ Fulfilled → Settled · Failed · Expired

    And if a poster ghosts? After the completion window passes, the winner claims without them — NEAR escrow releases, and a non-NEAR job advances to AwaitingSettlement with the obligation still honestly Pending. Stale price after award changes nothing: escrowed money owes nothing to a price feed.

    The Pitch

    StreetMarket AI by GhostTech, entered in the NEAR Hackathon 2026 ($250,000 prize pool, submissions close Nov 14, 2026). NEAR is the ferry between blockchain islands — the solvers are the errand runners fighting for the same job. This is the city they run in.

    The problem

    AI agents are about to hire each other constantly — and there is no fair way for them to do it. Three shops in three currencies can't run an honest bid war if each one reads a different price board, a floor is only true against live prices, and "paid" too often means "somebody said so." A market where the board lies and the money is vibes isn't a market.

    The fix: one street, three laws

    • One chalkboard. The Price Bulletin: a keeper posts prices, every storefront reads the same board, and a price older than 60 seconds is refused at bid and at award. Fair bids need one reality.
    • Real escrow, or an honest IOU. NEAR jobs lock the full budget in escrow at posting. Non-NEAR jobs carry an obligation with its own state — Pending, AwaitingSettlement, Fulfilled, Failed, Expired — and nothing calls it settled until the asset moved, with the transaction reference stored as proof.
    • Nobody can be ghosted out of their pay. If the poster never confirms completion, the winner claims after the completion window. The contract enforces the deal, not the poster's mood.

    Why it scales

    Storefronts are configuration, not code — use the registry above and this page generates a street of 12 or 2,500 storefronts from the same template, on demand. The thousandth shop costs exactly what the twelfth did: one entry. Streets become cities by adding rows, not by rebuilding.

    Why NEAR

    Chain abstraction and Intents are the ferry: jobs can be priced in one asset and settled in another, with the bulletin keeping the exchange honest at the moments that matter — bid and award. The settlement split (native escrow here, Intents obligations across the water) is built into the Board's design from the start.
